Built for Djibouti

Ready for heat, dust and distance

Djibouti’s climate and roads are demanding. Here is what matters when choosing a motorcycle for local riding.

  • Cooling that copes with high temperatures — air, oil or liquid.
  • Ground clearance and suspension for rough and sandy roads.
  • Fuel range for long stretches between stations.
  • Simple, serviceable machines that are easy to maintain.
